# Hooked on Wanting

An absurdist fishing game. The fish are drawn realistically. The lures are things *people* want: cash, cheeseburgers, beer, gold bars, diamond rings, sports cars, guitars, bicycles, crowns, yachts. Fish shouldn't want any of it. They bite anyway.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone, landscape works best). It needs no build step or dependencies.

## Loop
1. **Cast** (3rd-person view from the boat): hold CAST, release at the power you want.
2. **Underwater** (side-scroll): the lure is retrieved right to left toward the boat.
   - Left: **▲ / ▼** set the lure's depth.
   - Right: **Retrieve** (hold), **Attract** (jiggles the lure; a steady rhythm beats mashing), **Set Hook** (press when a fish bites).
   - Curious fish follow the lure. The ring over each fish shows how interested it is. When it fills, the fish strikes.
3. **Fight**: hold ▲ (or Retrieve) to reel, ▼ to give line, release to ease off. Keep the **tension** out of the red (the line snaps) and out of the yellow slack zone (the hook slips). Giving out too much line spools you.
4. **Sell and shop**: each catch pays by weight and species. Spend the money at the **Absurdity Lure Co.** on lures, rods (longer casts reach bigger fish), and line (needed for muskie and sturgeon).

Keyboard: W/S or ↑/↓ for depth · J/← retrieve · K attract · L/Space set hook · hold Space to cast.

Progress is saved in localStorage.
