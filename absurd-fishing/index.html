Hooked on Wanting

A peaceful fishing game, except every lure is something people want. Fish don't want any of it. They bite anyway.

Cast: hold the big button, then let go when the power is right.
▲ ▼ (left side): set the lure's depth.
Retrieve: reel the lure in, right to left. Fish follow it.
Attract: jiggle the lure. A steady rhythm works better than mashing.
Set Hook: press it the moment a fish bites.
Fighting: hold ▲ to reel in, ▼ to give line, let go to ease off. Watch the tension.
