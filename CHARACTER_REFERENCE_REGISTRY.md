# Character Reference Registry

Primary visual authority for every Rex Fleet character below is the **released
Issue 01 cast plate**, SSV `sites/visions/public/images/pages/rex-fleet/issue-01/page-003.jpg`
(byte-identical to RP approved draft `production/drafts/rex-fleet/rex-fleet-i01/rf_i01_cast.png`),
plus the released story pages noted. Text descriptions are RP
`data/shows/rex-fleet-s1/characters.json` (`visualAnchor`); per RP
`production/references/rex-fleet/visual-reference-pack.json`, **released art controls
and text supplements**. Game portraits are crops of page 003 produced by
`tools/import_reference_assets.py` (`assets/characters/*_256.png`, `*_96.png`) and are
flagged for owner review as derivative crops.

Chronology for all: Rex Fleet Season One, around Issue 01 (Reach and Resolve era).

## Playable explorers

### Commodore Ella Venn — The Fleet
- **Name status:** Ella Venn in all released and current production material; the owner's brief says "Ilyra Venn" (earlier edition). Open decision — `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md`.
- **Role:** "Fleet — Commander of Thunderbreak (Heavy Cruiser)". Relieved of command after Issue 02/03; Lt. Erin Deka commands Thunderbreak from Issue 04.
- **Appearance (text):** "Late-middle-aged woman with an angular, composed face and dark hair strongly streaked with silver, secured in a low command bun." **Seen:** page 003 portrait, cover left, pages 007, 019.
- **Clothing:** "Tailored deep-navy Fleet dress uniform with a high collar, restrained gold piping, star insignia, and small service ribbons."
- **Palette:** deep navy, gold, white. **Sigil:** gold four-point compass star.
- **Silhouette (ship):** Thunderbreak — long navy-and-white heavy cruiser hull with gold trim and blue engines (seen); bridge "repeatedly repaired rather than cosmetically renewed".
- **Personality:** "Calm, deliberate, unshowy; authority through steadiness and operational clarity."
- **Documented actions:** orders Thunderbreak to "match the wreck's drift… ease it into their path" to block raiders (page 007) — protection by positioning.
- **Relationships:** Admiral Cael Dominion (institutional antagonist), Naomi Sol, Lt. Erin Deka.
- **Game adaptation:** *Hold the Line* bulwark (protective positioning, consistent with page 007); heaviest hull, slowest. Ship drawn navy hull + gold trim + ion-white edge.

### Captain Naomi Sol — The Verge
- **Role:** "Verge — Escort commander"; damaged-corridor knowledge "from repeated real crossings".
- **Appearance:** "Lean woman with dark hair pulled into a functional braid, a visible brow scar…" **Seen:** page 003, cover centre, page 005 (cockpit), page 010.
- **Clothing:** "Patched tan-and-muted-green Verge escort gear." Leaf patch (cover).
- **Palette:** tan, muted green, gold accents. **Sigil:** leaf sprig.
- **Personality:** "Blunt, sharp, proud."
- **Released line (page 010):** "Three containers taken. One split. No one lost from the freighter."
- **Game adaptation:** fast escort fighter; *Corridor Run* dash; hazards hurt her less (corridor knowledge). Ship: tan hull, green trim.

### Tessa Banks ("Tess") — The Shards
- **Role:** "Shards — Captain of Gravefire Cutter"; Abby Saville's sister.
- **Appearance:** text "short-to-medium silver-gray curls"; **released art: wavy platinum-blonde hair** (page 003, page 021) — art controls.
- **Clothing:** "Pale Shard flight coat maintained through visible repairs." White/red coat.
- **Palette:** pale grey-white, bright red, black. **Sigil:** red fang.
- **Personality:** "Sparse, cutting, honest, weary of ideology."
- **Documented:** repairs signed off by Tessa (page 014: "Keep the pressure frame braced until Tessa signs it off").
- **Game adaptation:** repair/support (*Seal the Breach*); Gravefire = compact working cutter. Ship: pale hull, red seams.

### Abby Saville — The Shards
- **Role:** "Shards — Warlord of the Scythe of Saville"; leads Black Drift (page 021, page 014); later "breaks with Billie to protect civilians" (RP).
- **Appearance:** "Tall, athletic Black woman with dark brown skin, long tight braids pulled back cleanly." **Seen:** page 003, cover right, pages 014, 021.
- **Clothing:** "Structured Shard command coat built from weathered pale gray-white industrial layers, with narrow bright-red seams… over dark fitted armor." Red sigil on the coat (cover).
- **Personality:** "Sharp, direct, pragmatic; hates wasted words." "Commands through stillness, economical movement."
- **Game adaptation (provisional):** *Call the Captains* — summons allied Shard cutters for a short defence (Shard captains rallying to civilian defence is documented for Issue 03, an approved-draft source); trait rewards holding position ("commands through stillness"). Ship: pale grey-white command hull, red seams.

### Jex Marrin — Independent / Veil Market
- **Role:** "Independent broker — Veil Market"; knows "timing windows, shear markers, anchor behavior, traffic intelligence"; sister Tali.
- **Appearance:** text "older white-haired… short rough beard"; **released art + Keeper sheet `jex-marrin-acting-and-pose-study.png`: tousled brown curls, stubble** — art controls.
- **Clothing:** "Weathered Verge travel cloak with stitched pockets and route seals. Anomaly-glass beads sewn into the cloak."
- **Palette:** brown/bronze (Independent band, page 003). **Sigil:** compass-cross.
- **Personality:** "Smooth, sardonic, transactional."
- **Game adaptation:** broker economy (+Signal, free reroll); *Veil Step* uses the anomaly-glass beads as optical distortion (provisional interpretation of a documented costume detail).

## Adaptation constraints (all characters)

1. Portraits come only from released/approved art via the pipeline; no regeneration.
2. Ship sprites use each character's faction palette and sigil; sigils are never placed on another faction's ships.
3. Abilities may adapt but must not contradict voice/role (e.g. Venn never fights for spectacle; Shards are not villains).
4. No invented dialogue presented as quotes from canon characters; released lines only, otherwise narration.
